# Weather admission: the continuation policy reverses the apparent root preference

The repaired weather certificate enables native search on **all 48 retained
spread/trade trajectories**. With the same frozen roots, replies and RNG labels,
the Protect root's terminal finishes increase from **9/24 to 16/24** under
native follow-up. The attacking root remains **13/24**. There are no sampled
terminal losses. All 48 paths replay the recorded opposition schedules exactly.

This is evidence for a continuation-policy confound, not a strategic-weight
failure. The original Protect root was not demonstrated to be inferior. It
finishes three scenarios that the attacking root does not, while retaining all
13 of the attacking root's sampled finishes under native continuation. These
are conditional short-horizon outcomes, not general win probabilities or proof
that Protect is optimal against unrepresented counterplay.

## The precise admission failure

Read-only instrumentation reproduces **74 fallback occurrences** from the old
spread/trade run. The **48 unique native materialization calls** all reject
`unsupported-public-weather-plan` for the same reason:

- Public history continuously records rain from turn one, with complete
  residual boundaries. In one representative turn-four request, its certified
  age is three residual turns.
- The public opening actives are Garchomp/Rotom on our side and
  Metagross/Pelipper on the opposing side.
- A fresh hypothetical battle starts from the proposed current active pair.
  Pelipper is benched in that constructor, so the fresh field is dry.
- The old unsourced weather check requires the fresh constructor to already
  contain rain. It rejects the valid public clock before reconstructing it.

The native worker reads a hypothetical Pelipper with Drizzle and Focus Sash;
this is an approved candidate, not access to the actual opponent's private set.
The diagnostic does not alter admission, commands or policy decisions. Its raw
public/hypothetical traces remain local, with a compact hashed audit tracked.

## Bounded repair

For continuous opening rain or sand, the public planner can now carry the four
public opening identities alongside the already certified residual count. It
requires a turn-one move request with an empty opening event delta, a complete
continuous public timeline and complete upkeep boundaries. A later first
observation is not treated as an opening witness. Weather restarts continue to
use their existing explicit activation source certificate.

The native worker preserves its existing opening-weather certificate when it
matches. Otherwise, for this new cold-constructor domain, it requires a unique
weather setter among those public opening identities in the proposed world.
Missing/duplicate sources, multiple possible setters and an opening Trace
remain unresolved. A current active pair is never substituted for the opening
identities, and species alone does not certify an actual hidden ability.

Native `setWeather` and native residual handlers establish and age the clock.
No duration is copied from an actual battle or assigned to make a projection
pass. One important native detail surfaced in controls: `hasItem()` ignores an
inactive source. When replaying the certified past activation, the callback
temporarily uses its historical active flag so a benched or fainted Damp Rock
or Smooth Rock holder still produces its original eight-turn weather. The
present flag is restored immediately in `finally`; the source is not revived.
Extension-item transfers/removals remain unsupported.

Independent positive validation checks the resulting source belongs to the
public opening candidates and that the duration agrees with its hypothetical
item and certified elapsed residuals. Public observation and legal-menu checks
remain mandatory. The repair grants no negative authority over unrepresented
opponent worlds.

## Frozen four-cell result

Each cell reports **terminal wins / terminal losses out of 24 scenarios** after
the same additional completed turn. The explicit experimental Earthquake prior
profile remains exactly the same as the preceding coverage-control run. The
production prior catalogue is unchanged.

| Fixed spread/trade root | Frozen V2 follow-up | Public native follow-up |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Earthquake + Mega Gardevoir Protect | 9 / 0 | **16 / 0** |
| Earthquake + Mega Gardevoir Expanding Force | 13 / 0 | **13 / 0** |

Every trajectory has a completed native follow-up search. There are **51 search
occurrences, 30 unique computations and 27 changed action occurrences**, including
replacement decisions. Fewer total checkpoints are needed than in the former
74-fallback paths because the resulting play differs. This is not merely a
rejection-label change. No weather fallback remains in this retained case.

The Protect root gains **seven finishes and loses none** relative to its V2
continuation. One matched example changes `Earthquake + Mystical Fire` to
`Earthquake + Hyper Voice`, yielding a terminal win. All seven conversion
witnesses, their root replies, RNG labels and changed decisions are retained
in the compact comparison report. They were not chosen to form a new scorer.

The one-turn root utility still prefers Protect (1071.66 versus 994.21). That
preference is not contradicted by these improved continuations. The earlier
9-versus-13 result cannot, by itself, justify reducing material preservation
or adding a setup/attack bonus.

## Preserved controls and practical limits

The entire original **288-trajectory** matrix was rerun. The source audit
permits only three changed production files: the public weather plan,
positive field validation and native reconstruction worker. Frozen case
identities, exact roots, response lists, four coupled RNG labels and prior
profile are unchanged. All **144 frozen-V2 action/opposition trajectories**
match. The **96 native trajectories in the other two retained cases** also
match their previous actions and first/second-turn outcomes exactly.

The speed case still avoids all eight sampled V2 terminal losses, without an
eventual-win claim. The alternative-attacker case retains the same conditional
conversion witness. Across all three cases there are **124 completed search
occurrences, 80 unique search computations, 84 changed action occurrences and
62 fallbacks**; forced waits remain separate. Maximum observed unique decision
latency is about **1.078 seconds**, within the shared eight-second budget.
No strategic weights, response aggregation, search depth or 8/3 limits changed.

## Separate new-position safety check

Before inspection, a separate manifest freezes **12 newly seeded positions
from six fresh lineup pairs**, with a three-turn public frozen-V2 prefix and
the explicit experimental prior profile. They use familiar synthetic sets and
the same V2 opponent algorithm, so this is not independently authored opposition
or competitive validation. All cases are retained without resampling.

Two prefixes end before a decision can be probed. Of ten remaining positions,
eight fall back and **two complete native search**, both changing the action.
There are **124 paired immediate-outcome scenarios overall**, of which **32**
belong to actual native searches. The latter contain **zero additional or
avoided immediate terminal outcomes**. Their nonterminal decisions remain
ungraded; this is not evidence that all native changes are beneficial or safe
over a longer horizon. It is too little admitted evidence to estimate a
tactical error rate. Active Phantom Force/Stomping Tantrum/Electro Shot and
other public-effect domains remain closed rather than being relaxed for this
probe. These inspected cases now belong to development data.
