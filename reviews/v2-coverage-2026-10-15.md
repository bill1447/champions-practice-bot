# One-turn coverage ablation, unfamiliar sets, and turn-two admission

**Disposition: retain 8 candidates / 3 responses as the experimental baseline;
promote nothing.** One-turn horizon, native mechanics, position scorer, two opening
hypotheses, RNG sample, and eight-second budget remain fixed. No policy, evaluator,
prior catalog or reconstruction code was changed. Work is isolated on
`experiment/v2-coverage`; PR #249 remains at `e0ae723`.

## Factorial coverage experiment

72 new full battles were paired with the 24 saved one-turn controls. Only the
opening uses native search; every later action uses frozen V2. Cases/seeds/teams
are those already examined, so this is a diagnostic ablation, not fresh validation.
Each candidate expansion doubles the root cap; the own-choice cap for intervening
replacement requests stays two. Response expansion applies at the root and any
intervening replacement/pivot phase. These definitions are recorded before play.

| Root candidates | Responses | Wins | Actual opening reply included | Mean opening time |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 3 | 10/24 | 12/24 | 0.35 s |
| 16 | 3 | 10/24 | 12/24 | 0.72 s |
| 8 | 8 | 12/24 | 24/24 | 0.90 s |
| 16 | 8 | 5/24 | 24/24 | 1.96 s |

All 72 new searches completed; no deadline/branch/phase failures or budget overruns.
The maximum was 2.46 seconds. Candidate-only expansion changed **zero** selected
opening actions. Expanding responses changed 20/24; expanding both changed 24/24.
All joint commands submitted by V2 were from their authoritative legal menus.

The apparent response-only gain is not clean evidence: four games used the
independent opponent's explicit random legal fallback during forced replacement.
All four candidate-only wins occurred in affected pairs. Removing affected pairs
gives **8/20 versus the saved baseline's 10/20**, with two baseline-only wins and
zero response-only wins. This does not establish that fallback caused those wins;
it prevents counting them as reliable policy evidence.

The 16/8 arm has no opponent fallbacks: five wins versus ten, six baseline-only
wins and one expanded-only win. On the twenty pairs clean across all four arms,
wins are respectively **10, 10, 8, 4**. No expanded configuration earns promotion.
The cheaper 8/3 version remains the experimental control, not the public default.

## What this isolates

The audit confirms that candidate sets are nested, response sets are nested, and
every shared action has **identical** native utilities when only candidates are
expanded. Adding responses makes fixed-action worst-case utilities nonincreasing.
Thus the regression is not a changed scorer, accidental candidate-dependent
simulation, changed hidden hypotheses, or inconsistent budgets.

On this closed-family suite, expanding to eight responses includes the actual
recorded reply in every hypothesis in all 24 cases. Yet adding candidates then
selects higher modeled utilities and loses more games. The limitation is no longer
explained solely by missing the actual reply or missing an own action.

This implicates the **decision objective and its relationship to later play**:
position utility, pure worst-case response aggregation, narrow set/RNG coverage,
the short horizon, and the V2 policy used afterward. The experiment does not
uniquely identify a bad leaf weight or prove that a particular position feature
is responsible. More computation can optimize the present objective more
successfully while producing worse recorded gameplay.

## Sealed native witnesses

After recommendations were sealed, three actual opening positions were restored.
All four plans were branched against the recorded enemy reply using eight coupled
future samples. Live snapshots were used only by this diagnostic. These are
conditional first-decision witnesses, not win probabilities or optimality claims.

* Psychic/sand: 8/8 chooses Follow Me + Mystical Fire Mega; 16/8 chooses Follow Me
  + Hyper Voice Mega. The latter raises modeled worst-case utility from -995.19
  to -945.31, but against the recorded reply it leaves more enemy survivors on
  average (3.875 versus 3.25). The response-only full game wins but is affected
  by an enemy replacement fallback; the wider-candidate game loses.
* Ground/grass against rain: 8/8 chooses Protect + Wood Hammer; 16/8 chooses Rock
  Slide + Grassy Glide. Modeled worst utility rises from -59.30 to -54.38.
  Against the recorded reply, both KO one enemy in all eight samples, but the
  Protect line retains four own survivors while the wider line retains three.
  The Protect line wins the recorded full game; the wider line loses. Neither
  game has an opponent fallback.
* Armarouge/Indeedee: the narrow response model chooses Trick Room + Protect;
  the wider one chooses Psychic + Protect. The latter gets better immediate
  utility against the recorded reply (mean 26.31 versus -9.64), with four own and
  four enemy survivors under both plans. Nevertheless the Trick Room line wins
  the full game and the Psychic line loses. Favorable immediate utility is not
  sufficient position valuation.

These examples should become decision-quality cases, without adding unconditional
Protect, focus, or Trick Room bonuses to force their answers.

## Unfamiliar hidden-set test

The frozen 8/3 version then faced 24 fresh paired cases: **48 new full battles**,
fresh live/opponent RNG, and hidden item/move combinations outside both search
hypotheses. Search priors were not expanded to include the test sets. The live
driver alone receives the changed teams; the decision helper receives its own
team and the original public prior text. Every search completed, with no opponent
fallback or budget overrun.

**Frozen V2: 6/24. Native one turn: 8/24.** There are two native-only wins and zero
V2-only wins; the gain is one correlated side-reversed case. It is a small retained
signal, not broad generalization or competence. Familiar species, compositions,
abilities and point allocations remain; this tests item/move misspecification.
Fresh RNG also differs from the original suite, so changes in aggregate win rate
cannot be attributed solely to the prior mismatch.

Format-validated variants include Focus Sash / Swords Dance / Earthquake
Excadrill, Expert Belt / Ice Punch Tyranitar, Wacan Berry / Hydro Pump Pelipper,
Magnet / Thunderbolt Archaludon, Choice Scarf / Flip Turn Basculegion, Miracle Seed
/ Drain Punch Rillaboom, Charcoal / Taunt Incineroar, and Sitrus Berry Rotom-Wash.
Not every reserve is selected in every game. Initial proposed Assault Vest,
Safety Goggles, Body Press Archaludon and U-turn Incineroar combinations were
rejected by format legality checks before any test battle; legal alternatives
were frozen before play. No changes were made in response to game results.

## Early midgame availability

A separate public-only probe selected the first turn-two move checkpoint from
six familiar-family and six unfamiliar-family seed-zero p1 games. It initialized
the existing `BeliefDecisionEngine`, advanced it with known **own** actions and
sanitized observations, and requested admitted states at the checkpoint. Neither
actual enemy command nor battle RNG nor live snapshot was supplied to that
engine or the tactical evaluator. No new reconstruction logic was introduced.

**Usable admitted positions: 0/12.** The existing path returned:

| Rejection | Positions |
| --- | ---: |
| Unsupported public effect duration | 8 |
| Unsupported owned Unburden lifecycle | 2 |
| Unsupported Stomping Tantrum history/state | 2 |

All six familiar and all six unfamiliar positions were unavailable. These are
failures of the current admission path/configuration, not evidence that Showdown
cannot simulate those mechanics. The new tactical evaluator could not run at any
of these checkpoints, so **no midgame decision-quality or strength claim follows**.
The probe allows an eight-second existing preparation phase and a separate
eight-second tactical diagnostic budget; it is not a deployment latency test.

This is a concrete integration blocker. Better opening search cannot establish a
native-search player while the existing path supplies no usable state at these
ordinary turn-two positions. The public effect-duration history already present
in the observed prefix is the first bounded admission failure to investigate,
before redesigning belief reconstruction or adding inference machinery.
